Privacy Notice — Flowli

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Data Controller

Flowli is developed and operated by Flowli Test Operator, Italy.

For privacy-related inquiries, contact support@flowli.app.

2. Data Processing Model

Flowli stores your financial and budgeting data — accounts and balances, movements and transactions, categories and other budgeting data, forecasts and projections, and related app settings — locally on your device. Flowli does not upload this data to Flowli servers and does not operate a backend that receives it. Backup and export features create files in storage that you select and control on your device; they do not send those files to Flowli, and Flowli cannot access or recover your locally stored data or your backup and export files.

3. Alerts and Notifications

Flowli uses in-app alerts shown in the app inbox only. Alerts can be marked done or snoozed.

4. Permissions

Flowli requests only the permissions needed for enabled features, such as local storage and local backups. If a permission is not granted, related features may be limited.

5. Purchases

If you purchase Premium, purchases and subscriptions are processed and billed by the applicable store on your device. Flowli does not receive your full payment card details, and payment and subscription transactions are handled by the store rather than by a Flowli payment backend. The app may receive limited purchase and entitlement information from the store to determine your Premium status. Your financial and budgeting data remains local to your device, as described above.

6. Support Communications

If you contact Flowli support, your email address, message, and any information you choose to provide are used to respond to your request and related follow-up. Support communications are not used by Flowli for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making.

Please avoid including passwords, payment card details, or financial information that is not necessary for your support request.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and related follow-up.

7. Data Retention and Control

You control your data. You can edit or delete your data in the app using its data-management and reset features, and you can manage your files through your device’s file management. Uninstalling Flowli or resetting the app removes the data stored locally in the app, but it does not necessarily delete backup or export files you have created separately outside the app’s active data store.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Because Flowli stores your financial and budgeting data locally on your device and does not hold that data on a Flowli backend, you can access, correct, delete, export, and otherwise manage that data directly through the app and your device.

For personal data that Flowli actually receives, such as information you provide when contacting support, you may contact support@flowli.app to exercise any rights available to you under applicable data-protection law. Depending on the circumstances, these may include rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and data portability.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection supervisory authority.

9. Contact

Privacy inquiries: support@flowli.app
